# The Bungalow Between Worlds – Episode 1: Realistic Scene Prompts for Google Flow (10 s each)

20 scenes x 10 seconds = about 3 min 20 s. They follow Chapter 1 (Ordinary Days) page by page. Each scene has one idea, one camera move and one or two lines of dialogue.

## How to use

1. Paste the **Style**, **Characters** and (for bungalow scenes) **House** blocks first, then the scene prompt.
2. For consistent faces, make one realistic still of each friend and of the bungalow first. Use them as start frames or reference images (frames-to-video / ingredients) in Flow.
3. If Flow only gives 8 s per clip, generate 8 s and use Extend, or trim and slow down to fit. Make 2-3 takes of key scenes.
4. Voices can differ between clips. You can record or generate the dialogue separately and add it in your editor.

## Style block

```
Photorealistic live-action cinema, shot on ARRI Alexa 35 with 35mm anamorphic lenses, natural skin texture and pores, real fabric and wood detail, motivated practical lighting, shallow depth of field, subtle film grain, 16:9, 24 fps. Real Kerala locations. Adventure comedy that turns into a thriller. Characters keep identical faces, hair and clothes in every shot. No cartoon, no 3D-animation look, no text overlays.
```

## Characters block

```
STANY (24): Kerala man, confident wide grin, sunglasses pushed up on his head, tan short-sleeve shirt, big hand gestures.
RENJITH (24): Kerala man, deadpan half-lidded eyes, dark patterned shirt, arms crossed.
JESVIN (25): Kerala man, calm, curly hair, round glasses, short beard, olive hoodie.
HASHIR (26): Kerala man, curly hair, beard, dark floral shirt, camera around his neck, laughs when scared.
Four ordinary friends in their twenties, each with a backpack.
```

## House block

```
THE WHISPERING BANYAN: old two-storey heritage bungalow in Wayanad, Kerala, dark carved teak, pillared veranda, ivy and moss on the walls, a huge banyan tree behind it. Inside: warm lantern light, a stopped grandfather clock carved with banyan roots, a grand piano, old framed photos, polished dark wood floor.
```

## Scenes

### 1. The door at 3 AM (cold open)
```
Night, a dark old study. Four backpacked friends seen from behind, in a row, facing a tall wooden door carved with a glowing golden banyan tree; golden light spills over their silhouettes. Slow push-in from behind them. Close on Hashir, nervous half-smile.
Hashir (whispering): "Okay. Nobody say 'let's open it.'"
Stany (grinning, eyes lit gold): "...Let's open it."
Renjith (deadpan): "Of course."
Audio: low hum, a faint heartbeat. Caption on screen: none.
```

### 2. Fort Kochi tea stall
```
Bright morning, busy tea stall at Fort Kochi, Chinese fishing nets behind. The four friends around a table with chai. Stany stands, one fist raised. Medium shot, slight handheld.
Stany: "Gentlemen, I have a plan."
Renjith (deadpan): "Your last plan had a goat in it."
Hashir (rubbing his arm): "I still have the scar."
Jesvin (sipping chai): "It was a small goat."
Audio: street chatter, gulls, light playful music.
```

### 3. The booking
```
Close-up of a phone screen in Stany's hand: a listing "The Whispering Banyan Heritage Bungalow, Wayanad, 3 nights - 14,500 rupees, 4.9 stars". The friends lean in. Cut between faces.
Jesvin (eyebrow raised): "Why is it so cheap?"
Stany (smug): "It's called a deal."
Renjith (scrolling): "Only one review. 'Quiet. Very quiet.'"
Hashir (thumbs up): "Perfect. I hate noise."
A green BOOKED stamp appears on the phone. Light comic music.
```

### 4. The road to Wayanad
```
Sunrise, a green jeep on a winding hill road between tea plantations, mist in the valley (drone shot). Cut inside: Hashir in the back seat filming himself, Renjith in the passenger seat, Jesvin driving, Stany's hand out of the window making a peace sign.
Hashir: "Day one. Nothing can possibly go wrong."
Renjith (flat stare): "You've just doomed us."
Jesvin (calm): "Statistically, no."
Audio: engine, birds, upbeat road music.
```

### 5. The reveal
```
Late afternoon golden light. The four friends with backpacks at the bottom of the frame, looking up at the real bungalow: overgrown, dark wood, one upstairs window dark, a huge banyan tree behind. Slow upward tilt. Cut to Hashir pointing at a small green pond with a frog on a lily pad.
Stany (sweating): "...The photos were taken from a flattering angle."
Renjith (deadpan): "From another decade."
Hashir: "Look! The pool!"
Audio: wind, distant crows, comic sting.
```

### 6. Too clean
```
Stany turns an old brass key; the heavy door creaks open and warm lantern light spills onto the veranda. Reveal the hall: polished floor, lit lantern, grand piano, staircase, flowers in a vase. The four stand frozen at the door. Slow dolly in.
Jesvin (running a finger along a table): "Dust level: zero."
Renjith (sniffing): "Abandoned houses don't smell like jasmine."
Stany (arms wide): "Rustic charm!"
Audio: door creak, soft ambience.
```

### 7. The stopped clock
```
A tall grandfather clock in the hall, hands stopped at 3:00, banyan roots carved on the case. The friends gather around it; close on the carved words "It keeps time between what was and what still is". Then Hashir presses one piano key; a single note rings, everyone freezes.
Jesvin (serious): "A clock that's stopped at three. Always three."
Hashir (pale): "Cat."  Renjith: "There's no cat."  Hashir: "Then a cat-shaped ghost."
Audio: long piano note, silence.
```

### 8. Calm before the scare
```
Sunset through tall windows, the friends settling in. Stany dramatically opens the master bedroom door. Cut to the lounge: four friends around a low table with snacks and a lantern, laughing, warm golden light. Slow, relaxed push-in.
Stany: "Master room. Leader's privilege."
Renjith: "Leader of what?"
Audio: laughter, warm acoustic music.
```

### 9. The photos
```
The lounge wall with three old framed photos side by side, dated 2003, 2008, 2018. In each, four different friends stand in front of this same bungalow. Jesvin leans close, glasses on his nose. Slow push-in on the frames.
Jesvin: "Three groups. Same bungalow."
Hashir: "Other tourists! Popular place!"
Renjith: "Four people in each."
Jesvin (squinting, finger on the glass): "...Wait."
Audio: ticking, a low drone starts.
```

### 10. Fifth friend
```
Extreme close-up of the 2018 photo: in the upstairs window behind the four smiling friends, a thin dark human figure stands, faint but clear. Quick cuts to the same window in the 2003 and 2008 photos, each with the same figure. Nobody laughs; Renjith slowly turns toward Stany.
Jesvin (quiet): "Same window. Same figure. Fifteen years apart."
Hashir: "...Fifth friend. Always invited."
Renjith: "Stany. Read the reviews again."
Audio: music drops out, sub-bass rumble.
```

### 11. The journal
```
Hashir pulls an old leather journal with a banyan tree on the cover from a drawer. Close on the page: aged paper, an ink sketch of a figure in a window, the title "The Watcher". Cut across the four faces.
Jesvin (reading): "He is always there. In every visitor. He never harms. He only watches. But when the tree chooses, he comes down."
Jesvin: "New rule. Nobody goes anywhere alone."
Hashir: "Not even the bathroom?"  Renjith: "Especially the bathroom."
Audio: pages turning, low strings.
```

### 12. Midnight chime
```
Night, the lounge. Four friends in sleeping bags on sofas around one lantern. Hashir whispers to the ceiling. Then all four sleeping faces; Stany's eyes snap open. Cut to the dark hall: the stopped grandfather clock, hands at 3:00, its pendulum swinging in a gold glow. BONG... BONG... BONG. The four sit up, pale.
Hashir (whispering): "Good night, fifth friend."  Renjith (eyes closed): "I will end you."
Jesvin (whisper): "That clock is stopped."  Hashir: "I know. That's the problem."
```

### 13. Footsteps
```
The dark staircase seen from the bottom, slow footsteps echoing above, CREAK... CREAK. The friends huddle, Jesvin holds a flashlight. Stany, brave voice cracking, pushes Hashir forward. They climb in a line, Hashir in front, Renjith last.
Stany: "I'll lead."  Hashir (betrayed): "You said you'd lead!"
Renjith (last): "I hate all of you."
Audio: creaking wood, heartbeat.
```

### 14. The open door
```
A long dark upper corridor with old portraits. At the end, the east-wing door is ajar and a warm golden line of light falls across the floor. The four walk toward it; Stany pushes the door open slowly with one finger.
Jesvin (flashlight trembling): "I checked that door twice. It was locked."
Stany: "Then somebody has a key."
Renjith: "So it's a person. Great."
Hashir: "That's WORSE!"
```

### 15. Someone lives here
```
The east-wing study: warm lamp light, a carved desk, shelves of old books, a chair pulled slightly out, a long window overlooking the banyan tree. On the desk, two porcelain tea cups with steam rising. Jesvin touches one cup with a fingertip. The four slowly turn their heads toward the dark window. Final shot from outside the window looking in, a faint reflection of a tall dark figure on the glass.
Jesvin: "Still hot."
Audio: near-silence, soft ticking.
```

### 16. The fresh photo
```
Hashir picks up a small instant photo from the desk with a shaking hand. Close on the photo: the four friends in front of the bungalow with backpacks, taken from the window above; handwritten on the white border "Today. 4:12 PM."
Jesvin: "That's us. When we arrived."
Renjith (low): "We were alone."
Jesvin: "No. We weren't."
Stany (no joke left): "Okay. I read the reviews wrong."
```

### 17. The note
```
Stany lifts a tea cup. Under it, an old parchment note and a small bronze key. Close on the note, handwritten: "You found it. At the third hour, the next door opens. Do not be late. - The Watcher". Cut to Hashir backing away, then the four running down the stairs, flashlight beam bouncing, the clock chiming faintly behind them.
Renjith: "Not late for what?"
Hashir: "I'm leaving. I'm taking the jeep. I'm taking nobody."
```

### 18. No way out
```
The hall. Stany pulls the front door handle with both hands; the door does not move. Renjith kicks it, then hops holding his foot. Jesvin checks his watch. Cut to the four sitting in a circle on the study floor facing a blank wall; Hashir holds his camera on his knee. End on the wall clock: 2:59.
Stany: "It's not locked. It just... won't open."
Renjith: "OW. Wood. It's wood!"
Jesvin: "2:56. We have four minutes."
Hashir: "If I die, upload this."
```

### 19. Three o'clock
```
Close on the wall clock, the hands moving to 3:00; DONG. The friends' wide-eyed faces lit by a growing golden glow. The study wall splits open and a tall wooden door carved with a glowing golden banyan tree rises out of it, light flooding the room, the four in silhouette pressed together in the foreground.
Stany (whisper): "Okay. Nobody touch it."
Audio: DONG, DONG, deep rumble, choir swell.
```

### 20. The door opens (cliffhanger)
```
The carved door, light a little brighter. Extreme close-up of the brass doorknob slowly turning on its own. Cut across the four faces, then a wide shot of the four tiny, lit gold from behind by the opening door. Hold, then cut to black.
Hashir (trembling): "Guys... it's turning."
Jesvin: "Not from our side."
Renjith: "...Someone's opening it."
End card (added in edit): "THE DOOR IS OPENING..." / "END OF CHAPTER 01 - Ordinary Days".
```
