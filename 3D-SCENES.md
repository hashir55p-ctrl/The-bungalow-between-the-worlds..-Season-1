# Episode 1 – 3D Scene Plan for Google Flow (10 s each)

Build or render each scene as a simple 3D set, then use that render as the start frame in Google Flow so the video model turns it into realistic video. The 3D scene fixes the layout, the camera and where the characters stand. Flow adds realism, motion and sound.

The dialogue and mood for every scene are in [README.md](README.md). Paste the README's Style, Characters and House blocks before each Flow prompt below.

## Workflow (per scene)

1. **Block out the 3D set.** Use the "3D set" line below. Simple shapes and flat colours are enough. Place four stand-in characters (capsules or your Tripo/Meshy models) where "Blocking" says.
2. **Render two stills from the camera:** the start frame (0 s) and, if Flow offers an end-frame option, the end frame (10 s) from the "Camera" move.
3. **Generate in Flow** with the start frame and the "Flow prompt". The prompt always tells Flow to keep the 3D layout and camera but make everything photoreal.
4. **Make 2-3 takes**, keep the best, then cut and add voices, music and the title card in your editor.
5. If Flow only gives 8 s clips, generate 8 s and use Extend, or trim and slow down to fit 10 s.

## Shared line for every Flow prompt

```
Use the attached 3D render as the exact composition, camera position and character placement. Convert it to a photorealistic live-action shot: real skin, fabric, wood and foliage, natural light, subtle film grain. Do not change the layout, do not add cartoon or 3D-render look.
```

## Scenes

### 1. The door at 3 AM
- **3D set:** dark study, tall wooden door with a glowing golden banyan carving in the centre of the far wall, bookshelves at the sides, one warm gold light from the door.
- **Blocking:** four friends in a row, backs to camera, 3 m from the door.
- **Camera:** slow push-in from behind them over 10 s; last 2 s cut to Hashir close-up.
- **Flow prompt:** Night study, golden light from the carved door on four silhouettes, slow push-in, Hashir nervous. Dialogue from README scene 1.

### 2. Fort Kochi tea stall
- **3D set:** open tea stall with a wooden table, four stools, kettle counter, Chinese fishing nets and the sea behind.
- **Blocking:** three seated, Stany standing with a raised fist.
- **Camera:** medium shot, slight handheld drift, ends close on Renjith.
- **Flow prompt:** Bright morning tea stall at Fort Kochi, banter about the goat plan. Dialogue from README scene 2.

### 3. The booking
- **3D set:** a table with four people leaning in; a phone held in the centre.
- **Blocking:** Stany holds the phone, others lean over his shoulders.
- **Camera:** start close on the phone screen, pull back to the four faces.
- **Flow prompt:** Phone listing "Whispering Banyan Heritage Bungalow", faces react, BOOKED stamp. Dialogue from README scene 3.

### 4. The road to Wayanad
